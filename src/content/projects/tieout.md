I'm excited to share my latest project, [Tieout](https://tieout.ai). A verification tool that compares AI-generated reports against original data sources to catch hallucinations, verify accuracy, and ensure complete data integrity.

As AI-generated content becomes more common in business workflows, trusting the output is a real challenge. LLMs can fabricate statistics, misquote figures, or generate plausible sounding but incorrect data points. Tieout was built to solve exactly this problem.

## The Problem

Anyone who has used AI to generate reports from data knows the uncomfortable feeling of not being entirely sure the numbers are correct. AI models can hallucinate data, misinterpret figures, or simply get things wrong. Manually cross checking every data point in a generated report against the source documents is tedious and error-prone.

## How Tieout Works

The verification process is straightforward and follows three simple steps:

1. **Upload your source files** — Upload your original data in CSV, PDF, Excel, or other document formats. These serve as the source of truth.
2. **Compare automatically** — Tieout automatically extracts and compares numbers, percentages, and data points between the generated report and the source documents.
3. **Get detailed results** — Receive a comprehensive accuracy report with discrepancy highlights and confidence scores.

## Key Features

### Multi Format Support

Upload and parse CSV, PDF, Excel, and other document formats as your source of truth. This flexibility means Tieout fits into existing workflows regardless of how data is stored.

### Smart Comparison

The comparison engine automatically extracts and compares numbers, percentages, and data points between reports. No manual mapping required.

### Hallucination Detection

Identify fabricated data, incorrect statistics, and made-up citations in AI outputs. This is the core value proposition, catching the things that are hardest to spot manually.

### Link Verification

Validate URLs and references mentioned in generated reports against actual sources. Broken or fabricated links are flagged automatically.

### Audit Trail

Every verification is logged, providing a complete history for compliance and accountability. This is particularly important for regulated industries.
